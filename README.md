## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **BURNED HORIZONS**. This trainer adds optional gameplay assistance for BURNED HORIZONS, including action point control, injury protection, armor durability support, and expanded equipment capacity. It is designed for the game's single-player tactical sandbox and persistent operator systems.

**Target Audience:** Players who want more forgiving tactical engagements, easier experimentation with squad loadouts, and reduced penalties from the game's persistent injury and equipment systems.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Activate Unlimited Action Points before moving an operator. The option supports extended movement and additional actions during the current turn, while normal battlefield positioning, line of sight, suppression, and overwatch rules remain part of the tactical experience.

### Tools & Inventory

Use Expanded Carry Capacity while preparing rigs, pouches, plate carriers, and rucksacks. This provides more room for equipment without changing the game's loadout-focused inventory structure.

### World & Environment

BURNED HORIZONS takes place across persistent operational areas rather than a sequence of traditional mission screens. Trainer options remain active only for the current session and do not replace the game's sandbox progression, operational state, or squad persistence.

### Combat & Weapons

Enable Operator Injury Protection before entering combat when you want to reduce the consequences of realistic wounding. No Armor Degradation protects equipped plates during firefights, while action point control supports longer tactical turns involving movement, suppression, overwatch, and interrupts.
